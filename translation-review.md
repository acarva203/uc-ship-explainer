# Translation review

## Coverage

Spanish, Simplified Chinese, Vietnamese, Korean and Hindi cover the guide’s reading text, expanded benefits and waiver explanations, care choices and changing care paths, premium selections, cost calculator states, deadlines, calendar, glossary definitions, navigation and accessibility labels.

## Corrections

- Corrected insurance premiums, expired deadlines, academic seasons, medical visits, hospital admission, referrals and insurance payment limits.
- Removed code and SVG instructions mistakenly included in the original catalogs.
- Preserved insurer and service names, contact addresses, dollar amounts, percentages, dates and official source links.
- Replaced sentence fragments and appended English plurals with complete translated messages containing value placeholders.
- Localized spoken US-dollar values and calendar dates without changing the underlying calculations or deadlines.
- Prevented initial English state from deleting the requested URL language.
- Preserved language names in their own languages and translated the skip link, menus, groups, table descriptions, diagram descriptions and changing calculator accessible names.

## Verification

- Reviewed catalogs for completeness and terminology/meaning, including a second language-specific review pass.
- Exercised all care paths, student categories and both term systems in every language.
- Verified the maximum slider state reads $4,500 paid by the student and $25,500 paid by the plan for a $30,000 covered amount, with translated cap notices and accessibility descriptions.
- Checked saved URL language restoration, phone menus, expanded disclosures and tapped insurance definitions.
- Inspected desktop and 320px layouts; no page-wide horizontal overflow or browser exceptions were observed.
- Automated accessibility checks returned only inline insurance definition target-size warnings, covered by the WCAG 2.5.8 inline-text exception.
- 26 regression tests passed, including locale preference, whole-message rendering, cost-value preservation and localized calendar dates.
